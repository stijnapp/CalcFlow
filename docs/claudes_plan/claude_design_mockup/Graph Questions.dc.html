How a drawn answer gets submitted, revealed and graded

Built on the shipped layout: tool rail plus 62% canvas on the tablet, stacked canvas with a bottom sheet on the phone, confidence captured before the reveal. The answer is overlaid on your sketch, and the machine-checkable parts are pulled out into real fields.

1a

Overlay reveal, checkable anchors, three-way self-grade

Your flow with two changes. The answer curve draws itself over your sketch rather than beside it, so comparing is spatial instead of remembered. And every graph question also asks for two or three typed key features — asymptotes, intercepts, components, magnitude — which the engine grades for real. The sketch stays self-graded, but on three levels, so it lands in the same correct / near / wrong buckets the rest of the app already uses. Pure self-grading would make Ch 5 and Ch 12 the only chapters whose mastery and speed numbers you cannot trust.

WHAT BOTH NEED FROM THE ENGINE

A graph question is a new problem shape: the generator has to emit a plottable reference (function plus window, or a list of lattice vectors) instead of only LaTeX, and the canvas needs a coordinate surface with real axes rather than the plain or dotted paper. The key-feature fields reuse the existing answer machinery, so correct / near / wrong keeps meaning what it means everywhere else in the app.
